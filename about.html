about our website

helping student make informed educational and career decisions

who we are

the sierra leone university & career guide website was created to provide students with valuable information about universities, departments,courses and career opportunities available in sierra leone.

the problem

Many students choose courses without understanding the career opportunities available after graduation. This often leads to uncertainty and poor career decisions.

our solution

tour platform connects students with information about universities, departments and courses while also showing possible job opportunities and career paths for each course.

our mission

To help students make better educational and career choices through easy access to reliable university and career information.

our vission

To become the leading online platform for university and career guidance in Sierra Leone.
